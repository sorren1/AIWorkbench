# Customer Resolution Policy 2026.2

**Policy ID:** POL-2026.2

**Effective:** 2026-07-01T00:00:00Z

## Expedite surcharge disputes

A posted expedite surcharge is eligible for a credit when the shipment is delivered more than 24 hours after the committed delivery time and the reviewed primary cause is carrier-caused. Carrier capacity failure, carrier equipment failure, and carrier routing failure qualify.

A customer-caused delay, invalid address, customer hold, customs hold caused by missing customer documentation, or an unknown/unreviewed cause does not qualify for automatic proposal. Conflicting or incomplete evidence must be escalated.

The eligible amount is the posted expedite charge for the disputed invoice, provided no prior credit exists for the same charge and case.

## Approval

- Credits up to and including USD 1,000 require Supervisor approval.
- Credits greater than USD 1,000 require Finance Manager approval.
- The proposer may not approve the proposal.
- Approval expires after 24 hours or when the evidence/proposal changes.

## Execution

Approved credits must include the case and proposal external reference. Duplicate external references must return the prior credit rather than create a new one. If finance succeeds but the CRM case update fails, the case must enter reconciliation and the finance credit must not be repeated.
