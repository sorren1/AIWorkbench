# Rehearsal Scorecard

Score each category 1–5 after a recorded rehearsal.

| Category | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Time control | >50% over | slightly over | ends on time with room for questions |
| Business framing | feature tour | problem and outcome present | outcome, constraints, and trade-offs lead |
| Technical precision | vague/overclaimed | mostly correct | clear implemented/recorded/design boundaries |
| CData understanding | connector name-dropping | data-access value explained | connectivity, context, control, and field role integrated |
| Customer orientation | internal engineering only | some stakeholder view | customer trust, handoff, and outcome explicit |
| Principal leverage | personal build | reusable patterns mentioned | clear field-to-product assets and organizational leverage |
| Concision | long monologue | mostly structured | conclusion first, evidence, stop |
| Interruption recovery | loses place | can resume | tour is intentionally interruption-safe |
| Truthfulness | boundaries blurred | minor ambiguity | production/local/synthetic distinctions explicit |
| Audio/share quality | hard to hear/read | usable | clear voice, readable at Teams share scale |

Release target: no category below 4 for the relevant stage.
