# Stage 5 — Executive / CPTO Lens

## Objective

Demonstrate principal-level judgment and field-to-product leverage. Keep answers short.

## Ninety-second version

> The customer problem required CRM, billing, operations, and policy evidence. I used Connect AI as the consistent data layer and placed a deterministic evidence, approval, and idempotent-action workflow above it. The important output is not one dispute demo; it is a reusable resolution blueprint—discovery questions, source mappings, tool contracts, policy/evaluation cases, approval and reconciliation patterns, support runbooks, and an evidence-backed product-friction log. A second customer changes mappings and policy rather than rebuilding the control architecture.

## Three-minute view

1. Outcome and architecture thesis.
2. Reusable versus customer-specific matrix.
3. Evidence-backed product friction and classification: configuration, documentation, FDE pattern, or platform opportunity.
4. Next-customer acceleration and expansion hypotheses.

## Executive answer format

- one-sentence conclusion;
- one concrete example;
- one measured outcome;
- one strategic implication.

## Avoid

- a four-minute code tour;
- claiming to know CData's internal roadmap;
- presenting public docs friction as a product defect;
- confusing bespoke consulting output with reusable platform IP.
