# Stage 4 — Customer Experience and Handoff Lens

## Objective

Show that the customer-facing organization can trust, explain, support, and own the result after the initial implementation.

## Ask before presenting

> I built the operating and handoff view for the same synthetic case. Would a brief walkthrough of how Support and Customer Success would receive and operate it be useful?

## Three-to-five-minute order

1. Customer-visible outcome and current action status.
2. Failure category and customer impact.
3. Ownership matrix: customer data owner, FDE, Support, Product, and Customer Success.
4. Runbook and escalation path.
5. Customer communication rule: never outrun receipts.
6. Acceptance, training, known limitations, and change process.

## Language boundary

> My background has been more product and platform engineering than formal consulting, but I have repeatedly translated ambiguous operational needs, external-system constraints, and stakeholder expectations into production architecture. I am intentionally extending that operating model into direct strategic customer ownership.

## Likely questions

- How do you run discovery without jumping to a solution?
- How do you handle an unhappy customer during an outage?
- How do you distinguish product, implementation, configuration, and data-quality failures?
- What does a clean Support/CS handoff contain?
- How do you communicate risk without blocking progress?
- How do you prevent yourself from becoming the permanent bottleneck?
