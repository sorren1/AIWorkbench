# Responsive and Presentation Behavior

## ≥1280px

- Two-column information layout allowed.
- Fixed/contained tour rail may remain visible.
- Architecture and evidence details can appear side by side.

## 768–1279px

- Primary content remains full width.
- Secondary details collapse beneath summaries.
- Tour rail becomes top progress bar or drawer.

## 320–767px

- Single column.
- Tables use labeled card transformation where meaningful; otherwise explicit horizontal region.
- Timeline becomes vertical.
- No hover-only content.
- Presenter controls remain sticky but compact.

## Interview share-screen mode

- Avoid content hidden below an unnecessarily tall hero.
- Critical status and disclosure visible without scrolling.
- Use 16:9-friendly page sections.
- Support browser zoom at 110–125% for Teams sharing.
