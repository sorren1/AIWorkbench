# Four Stage View Specifications

## Stage 2 — Strategy

Primary question: “Can this person turn a vague customer objective into a scoped engagement and architecture?”

Required panels:

- problem statement and business outcome;
- discovery question sample;
- source authority matrix;
- architecture with control boundaries;
- assumptions/risks;
- acceptance criteria;
- phased rollout;
- 3-minute tour.

## Stage 3 — Technical

Primary question: “Did this person build and understand the system?”

Required panels:

- connection/schema discovery;
- query catalog and purpose-specific service tools;
- canonical evidence packet with provenance;
- structured-versus-RAG decision;
- policy evaluator;
- approval digest;
- unauthorized write rejection;
- idempotency replay;
- partial failure and trace;
- tests/evals and 7-minute tour.

## Stage 4 — Operations

Primary question: “Can customers and customer-facing teams trust and operate it?”

Required panels:

- customer-visible case state;
- failure-category decision tree;
- source and workflow health;
- RACI/ownership;
- support handoff packet;
- escalation/communication timeline;
- known limitations and change process;
- 3–5 minute tour.

## Stage 5 — Product

Primary question: “Does field work create reusable platform leverage?”

Required panels:

- one-sentence outcome;
- reusable blueprint layers;
- customer-specific versus reusable matrix;
- product-friction items with evidence;
- next-customer acceleration model;
- expansion hypotheses;
- 90-second and 3-minute tours.
