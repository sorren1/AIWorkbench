# UX Brief

## Experience objective

A senior interviewer should understand the customer problem, control model, and outcome within 30 seconds, then choose the depth relevant to the conversation. The interface is an architecture case study with working replay—not a consumer dashboard and not a chatbot.

## Primary navigation

- Strategy
- Technical
- Operations
- Product

The route navigation may appear inside CaseFlow, but no navigation to CaseFlow may be added elsewhere on the portfolio.

## Persistent shell

- CaseFlow title and independent-project disclosure.
- Current lens and tour duration.
- Mode badge: deterministic replay, recorded live, or local live.
- Claim legend.
- Reset canonical case.
- Presenter mode and keyboard help.

## Core visual primitives

- Source map
- Evidence packet
- Decision/policy card
- Approval boundary
- Action/receipt timeline
- Failure and reconciliation panel
- Support handoff
- Reuse/product-learning matrix

## Interaction priorities

1. Direct deep links work.
2. Guided tour can pause for questions and resume.
3. Important evidence is inspectable without modal nesting.
4. No animation is required to understand state.
5. Replay controls never visually impersonate a real source write.
