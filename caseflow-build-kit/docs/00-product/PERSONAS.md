# Personas and Jobs to Be Done

## Customer Resolution Analyst

- Needs one trustworthy case view.
- Must see source facts and missing evidence.
- Can draft but cannot exceed financial authority.
- Needs a customer-facing explanation that cites the evidence basis.

## Financial Approver

- Needs proposed amount, policy clause, evidence digest, risk/conflict state, and execution scope.
- Approval must be explicit, attributable, time-bound, and tied to the exact proposal.

## Support / Customer Success Manager

- Needs customer impact, current state, next owner, known limitations, and communication history.
- Must distinguish recoverable source failure from product defect or scope mismatch.

## FDE / Implementation Engineer

- Needs source inventory, mappings, data owner, auth model, query/tool contracts, acceptance criteria, runbook, and observability.
- Must turn customer-specific choices into reusable patterns.

## Product / Platform Leader

- Needs evidence of recurring field friction, reusable assets, product gaps, and time-to-next-deployment.
- Does not need a UI feature tour.

## Security / Data Owner

- Needs identity, scope, retention, PII classification, action authorization, auditability, and failure/rollback behavior.
